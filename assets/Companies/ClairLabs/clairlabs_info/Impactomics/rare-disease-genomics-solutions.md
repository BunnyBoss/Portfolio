## Offerings and Key Features

NGS Secondary Analysis

Accepts FASTQ/VCF from WGS, WES, and targeted panels inputs across cloud-
native and hybrid environments. Automated QC and alignment run without manual
configuration, thus supporting probands, family, and large cohorts
reproducibly.

Phenotype based gene prioritization

Clinical teams enter symptoms using HPO-linked terms, mapped in real time to
OMIM, HPO, and other curated sources. Gene candidates surface ranked by
phenotype relevance even before the variant list is reviewed.

Variant Ranking

Our advanced ML model scores each variant for pathogenicity probability and
ranks candidates with supporting annotations and evidence from 40+ sources,
including ClinVar, LOVD, population databases like gnomAD, along with
proprietary South Asian-specific MAF and reported variants – giving analysts a
complete picture, not a raw score.

ACMG-based Variant classification

Automated ACMG classification applies to PVS1, PS1, PM2, and all standard
evidence rules to every variant. CNVs are classified as per Clingen-ACMG.
Curators retain full override capability, and every decision is logged for
audit and compliance.  

Family Analysis

Trio and family-based pipelines detect de novo variants, apply inheritance
filters, and support carrier screening — all within a single analysis run.
Improve diagnostic yield without additional analyst effort.  

Reporting

Clinicians receive a structured PDF or WORD report with evidence summaries,
classification rationale, and variant – Disease interpretation that is
version- controlled, customizable, and fully traceable to source data.

## How Do We Stand Apart from Competitors?

Phenotype-first  
Intelligence

Impactomics starts with the patient's clinical presentation and not the
variant list. HPO profiles, OMIM, ClinGen, and Orphanet drive prioritization
before manual review begins.  

AI Evidence Synthesis  
at Variant Level

For every candidate variant, Impactomics auto-generates an explainable
evidence summary, drawing on ClinVar, PubMed, ClinGen, and functional studies,
so that analysts review conclusions rather than raw sources.

Explainable ACMG  
Classification

Every ACMG criterion is backed by source evidence, applied logic, and
supporting data. No black-box labels, ensuring full auditability for CAP/CLIA-
compliant sign-out.

Integrated Family-based  
Analysis

Singleton, duo, trio, and extended pedigree analyses run natively, including
de novo detection, compound heterozygosity, and segregation evidence, are
resolved without external tools or manual data merging.

AI-assisted Variant  
Quality Verification

Integrated BAM assessment agents evaluate read depth, allele balance, strand
bias, and mapping quality. Flag potential artifacts before interpretation,
reducing false positives at the source.

Unified Rare Disease  
Knowledge Graph

Impactomics connects patient phenotypes, genes, diseases, variants,
literature, and clinical evidence into a single dynamic graph. Teams find
clinically relevant associations that simple variant filtering consistently
misses.
