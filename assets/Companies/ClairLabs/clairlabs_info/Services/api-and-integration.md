## Service Overview

Automation and APIs promise scalable, interoperable genomics and lab
operations, driven by rapid FHIR adoption and surging lab-automation
investment. However, integration complexity, skills shortages, and lengthy
implementations slow adoption. Regulators now demand lineage, privacy, and
reproducibility across pipelines.

ClairLabs delivers API-first LIMS/instrument integration, omics workflows,
governed ETL, automated QC, and lineage pipelines. Effortlessly produce AI-
ready, audit-grade datasets for diagnostics, biopharma, and clinical research.
