### FAQs

How do you protect patient data?__

HIPAA-compliant data protection with end-to-end encryption, role-based access,
immutable audit logs, and automated DLP. This ensures secure PHI handling and
enterprise-grade data governance.

Can you integrate with our EHR/LIS?__

Yes. FHIR-enabled, HL7-compatible interoperability with scalable REST APIs and
secure connectors leads to real-time EHR/LIS integration and seamless data
exchange.

Are pilot KPIs guaranteed?__

No. KPIs are presented as pilot-based targets and evidence-driven benchmarks;
outcomes vary by site and data quality, but pilots are optimized for
measurable, outcome-focused results.

Who should join the Discovery call?__

We welcome cross-functional leaders, such as CMO, Clinical Lead, Head of
Operations, IT/System Architect, and a data or lab lead, to align clinical
goals, integration scope, and success metrics.
