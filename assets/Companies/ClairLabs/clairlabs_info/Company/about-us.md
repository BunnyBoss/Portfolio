# About Us

## Turning Potential Into Progress, One Outcome at a Time

##### "Innovation in science is all about bold ideas punctuated with
breakthroughs."

ClairLabs began as a small hub of a cross-disciplinary team including domain
experts, bioinformaticians, data engineers, and cloud architects. We were
driven by a single conviction – scientific discovery should be faster, more
reproducible, and accessible through intelligent systems.

Within a year, we’ve scaled that conviction into end-to-end platforms,
validated NGS pipelines, and AI solutions fueling genomics to multi-omics at
enterprise scale. Today, we blend domain rigor with software engineering
discipline to deliver tools and services that move research from “what if” to
“what works.”

## Chandra Ambadipudi

#### Co-Founder, ClairLabs

Chandra is a serial technology entrepreneur with a proven track record of
building and scaling data-driven ventures. Throughout his career, Chandra has
focused on solving hard, foundational problems — the kind that require long-
term thinking, technical depth, and disciplined execution. Rather than chasing
trends, he has consistently gravitated toward complex, high-stakes domains
where intelligent systems can unlock transformative value.

Prior to founding ClairLabs, he led successful entrepreneurial initiatives
delivering advanced data and engineering solutions to global enterprises.

Chandra brings to ClairLabs a rare blend of entrepreneurial resilience,
engineering discipline, and forward-looking AI strategy, anchored in the
belief that the most meaningful companies are built by taking on problems that
matter for decades, not quarters.

Under his leadership, the organization is building AI-native platforms and
engineering capabilities designed to address some of the most difficult
challenges in modern medicine — rare diseases, precision diagnostics, and
scalable interpretation of high-dimensional biological data. His long-term
vision is to position the organization as a trusted innovation partner for
organizations tackling the most consequential problems in science and
healthcare.

## Pankaj Gaddam

#### Co-Founder, ClairLabs

Pankaj is a visionary technology leader with more than two decades of
experience transforming enterprises through data, artificial intelligence, and
intelligent automation.

At ClairLabs, Pankaj leads technology strategy and platform engineering to
build next-generation, AI-powered genome data analysis systems. He is driving
innovation across clinical genomics, biomarker discovery, multi-omics
integration, and population genomics—empowering diagnostics labs, biotech
companies, and research institutions to accelerate scientific breakthroughs.

His vision centers on harnessing AI to decode biological complexity at scale,
democratize precision medicine, and fundamentally reshape how genomic insights
translate into real-world patient impact.

An entrepreneur at heart, Pankaj previously founded and led successful
ventures focused on modern data and analytics solutions for large enterprises.
He has worked closely with Fortune 500 organizations to architect and deliver
scalable platforms that convert complex data into strategic advantage. His
work consistently bridges deep technical expertise with business strategy,
enabling organizations to modernize, innovate, and lead in their industries.

## Innovating Science Through Diverse Perspectives

We believe breakthrough discoveries happen when diverse minds collaborate. DEI
at ClairLabs is not an add-on – it is the heart and soul of our scientific
mission. Our key pillars include:  

Neurodiversity & Cognitive Variety

We design roles and processes that let neurodivergent talent contribute at
full strength, valuing different thinking styles as a source of scientific
advantage.  
  

Global Perspectives, Local Impact

We recruit across geographies so that a developer in Bengaluru and a
biostatistician in Chandler bring complementary context to the same problem.  
  

Gender Equity in STEM Leadership

We actively close representation gaps—from cloud engineering to principal
scientists—so leadership reflects the global science community we serve.  
  

Inclusion in Action

We embed inclusion into hiring and daily work through bias-aware assessments,
flexible work models, and continuous learning that strengthens collaboration
across teams.  

Our commitment centers on three interconnected dimensions: people, processes,
and platforms — shaping hiring, how teams work, and how we partner with
clients and communities.
